At the Academic Awards Night on April 23, 2026, multiple of The Cornellian Executive Board members and writers won awards for academic excellence. Congratulations to James Belding, who won the Maxwell Miller Jr Award in history; Murray Page, who won the John Cotton Brown Award in politics and the John Dean Award in environmental justice; Rhye Nelson, who won the Madonna Cutsinger Award and the Winifred Van Etten Award in English; Adelaide Ries, who won Barbara Gail Lieberknecht Award for English and an Honorable Mention for the Tom Garst Poetry Prize; Jack Gross, who won Senior Award for theatre; and more! Congratulations to the over 100 award winners across every academic department for their excellence.
