---
title: Is the Former Hotel Roosevelt Haunted?
author: V Marty
pubDate: Oct 7, 2026
---

Though the Iowa countryside isn’t a common destination for ghost hunters, it isn’t entirely devoid of grisly murders and unexplained occurrences. A search for the supernatural close to home brought us to Cedar Rapids’ Roosevelt Apartments, formerly Hotel Roosevelt.

For decades after its construction in 1927, the Roosevelt was considered one of the most luxurious hotels in Iowa. However, its reputation was stained by the murder of Byron Hattman in 1948, and stories of inexplicable events have haunted it ever since. In an effort to find out if these rumours were true, we investigated the complex, now refurbished, apartments.

After taking an elevator to the seventh floor, where the murder happened, the dim lights and narrow hallways gave us the immediate feeling that we weren’t welcome. The corridors were empty and the doors we knocked on offered us no response, so we quickly returned to the lobby.

There, we interviewed a resident who’d been living in the Roosevelt for a year. They told us about yet another murder, this one on the second floor, and the tall, shadowed figure they sometimes saw near the second floor’s laundry room. They also mentioned items falling off shelves and the door to their room slamming shut, seemingly on its own. Other past residents have shared similar stories online, and though none of them can be confirmed, they paint a fascinating picture.
