Apparently, you can spell “orgasm” without “Go Rams.”

Stepping into the hazy, vaguely fungal funk of Tarr Floor 3, I could sense right away that something was awry. It was dark, a lone light barely flickering in the distance, the shadows on the doorways clutching at the corners of my eyes. It was quiet. Too quiet.

My anonymous tip had informed me that condoms on the floor were mysteriously vanishing by the bowlful. Knowing that the residents of Tarr 3 weren’t getting action, it was understood that the purloined packets must be going elsewhere. I, Engelbert Tush, the best private eye in rural Iowa, was on the case like a fat broad on a donut.

I pulled a cigarette out of my pocket and lit it. As I took a drag, I noticed the green mist and flies drifting lazily from the bathroom. This was a situation that needed to be investigated. I crept into the bathroom and noticed a plastic bin with one single silver packet in it through my watering eyes, which had instantly fogged up thanks to the unique odor of burnt popcorn, stale vomit, and Bo Burnham CDs. I soldiered through the nauseating atmosphere, only momentarily soothed by the pleasant aroma of tobacco and tar radiating from my smoke. Above the bin, I encountered a poster angrily asking for condom takers to identify their home floor with a corresponding tally mark. A survey. I’ve hated surveys ever since they killed my girl, Puss. A tear sprang to my eye. Oh, sweet Puss. My angel Puss.

I wiped my eyes and peered at the silver foil. A condom, eh? I haven’t much experience with them, preferring instead to use my trusty leather glove, one finger at a time, but from my understanding they happen to be a precious and scarce commodity. Looking closer, the label read XL in large letters. It appeared the thieves were packing pocket pistols instead of tommy guns.

“I’ve got a hot tip for you.” whispered a faint voice in my ear.

Now the last time I heard that phrase, there was a hole cut out in the stall where I was taking a crap, so I was petrified. I spun on my heel to see nobody.

“Look up.” My gaze flicked up to the shadowy ceiling, where there was still nothing.

“Look closer.” I zeroed in on a cluster of dark squiggles. Worms.

“Bingo. Now, we didn’t get a good look at anyone’s faces, but we can say with fair certainty that these are the same individuals who tore the toilet seat off its hinges. And planted mold in the walls. And flooded the bathroom floor permanently. And-”

“Alright buster, I get it.”

“This could only be the work of one group.”

“Italians,” I said at the same time as the worms said, “Ghouls.”

“Italians? Dude, that’s like 1890s level racism. They're ghouls.”

Now, I don’t see the difference personally, but the worms seemed offended, so I canned it.

“How do we figure out who the ghouls are?” I asked the ceiling worms.

“You’re the detective,” replied the worms.

From the evidence I collected, the bandits’ worms were even smaller than the ones in the ceiling. There was only one solution.

“I got it! Let’s line everyone up and measure their-”

“Nope. Nope. I’m gonna stop you right there. That definitely violates Title IX. And also ew. They’re ghouls. They hate bright light.”

After a period of reflection and a brief moment of silence for Puss, I hatched my brilliant plan.

“Urethra!” I laid out a tripwire over the condom box, connected to an impossibly bright bulb, which contains enough wattage to vaporize a dozen intruders. I departed Tarr, confident in my plan. Another job well done. Now, off to the bar.

> Editor’s note: A resident of Tarr was temporarily blinded after reaching for their toothbrush without looking one night. The trap was immediately deconstructed by custodial staff. No suspects were apprehended. The worms are still in the bathroom ceiling. The XL condom remains unused.
