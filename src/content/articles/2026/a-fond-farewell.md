---
title: A Fond Farewell
author: James Belding
pubDate: Wed Apr 29, 2026
---
I have been part of The Cornellian for my entire career at Cornell. The Cornellian and the students who were leaders of the newspaper at the time were the reason I chose to come to Cornell College. As a freshman, I was searching for a place to belong, and The Cornellian became one of the most important parts of my life. I have been honored to become a co-editor of The Cornellian; the leadership of The Cornellian when I first joined were inspirational and I hope that as of this point, I have succeeded in continuing their work. I am looking forward to The Cornellian’s future after I graduate, knowing the newspaper is in good hands. The Cornellian has grown exponentially since my first year here and I hope that growth will continue until the newspaper has become a central part of campus life. I know that Murray and Rhye will do an excellent job of making sure the newspaper grows, and I am excited for the future of the newspaper under their leadership.
