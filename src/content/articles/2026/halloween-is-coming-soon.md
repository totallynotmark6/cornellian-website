Halloween is coming up, and here at Cornell we are blessed, or maybe just lucky enough to have the block plan. And what does that mean? Block breaks. So, if you are sitting in class, staring at the clock, wondering what you are going to do with your next break or maybe just the weekend, why not get into the Halloween spirit?

Whether you are looking for something terrifying or just want to spend an afternoon doing something fun, there are plenty of Halloween activities around Iowa. Here are a few options worth checking out.

If you are the type of person who thinks being scared is a fun weekend activity, **Circle of Ash** might be for you. Starting October 2, this seasonal haunted attraction at the Linn County Fairgrounds in Central City, Iowa will be open on weekends for anyone brave enough to enter.

Tickets range from around $31 for general admission to $83 for the VIP experience, so you can choose how much you are willing to pay to voluntarily get scared. Circle of Ash includes three main attractions: Frightmare Forest, an outdoor haunted trail; The Manor, an indoor haunted house; and Socko’s Fun House, a fog filled clown maze. So, if you are feeling brave, I encourage you to take this challenge.

But hey, maybe being chased by monsters is not exactly your idea of relaxation. If you want something more peaceful, **Bass Family Farm** offers a great fall alternative.

If you’re looking for something less scary, Bass Family Farms is a great fall activity in Mount Vernon. Located right across from Palisades-Kepler State Park, this family owned farm is a popular local destination during the fall season.

From September to October, visitors can enjoy a pumpkin patch filled with all shapes and colors, a corn maze where you’ll lose track of time, tractor rides if you are feeling bold, and much more. You can also stop by the farm store for local products: honey, jams, and even 16 flavors of Wisconsin ice cream. While admission to the pumpkin area and store are free, the other activities require a wristband.

If haunted houses aren’t your thing, grab some friends, pick a pumpkin, and get lost in the corn maze instead!

Last, but definitely not least, is **Bloomsbury Farm** in Atkins, Iowa. The farm is known for its seasonal festivals, especially the Iowa Fall Festival.

During the fall, visitors can explore an 18-acre pumpkin patch, a 10-acre corn maze. Along with more than 30 family friendly attractions, including giant slides, jumping pillows, tractor rides, a petting farm, and minigolf.

But, once the sun goes down, Bloomsbury gets a little scarier with Scream Acres, featuring haunted houses and a terrifying corn maze during October weekends.

So, whether you want to scream, get lost, pick pumpkins, or simply escape campus for a few hours, this Halloween season has something for everyone. Now, you just have to convince your friends to come with you, or maybe go by yourself. I mean, that is, if you are not scared, which I hope you are not!
