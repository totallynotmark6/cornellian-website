---
title: The Lincoln Highway Arts Festival
author: Murray Page
pubDate: Oct 7, 2026
---

The Lincoln Highway Arts Festival took place on First Street in uptown Mount Vernon on September 26th. Each artist had their own booth or shared a booth with a partner. The pieces present included painting, collage, printmaking, ceramics, and stained and fused glass. I loved getting to wander through the booths, look at artwork, chat with the artists, and see community members and Cornell students do the same. There was a live band playing in front of the old school and food trucks up the street, making the festival even more lively.

Artist, Nadine Mitchell, won best in show. She featured work in both oil paint and in collage. My favorite work of hers was four oil paintings picturing four different women all wearing the same white dress with beautifully textured flowers. When I asked Nadine about the pieces, she explained that the dress pictured is her wedding dress. She was tired of it sitting in a box and originally was going to paint a self-portrait of herself wearing it. However, she didn't like the idea of looking at a picture of herself as she painted, so instead she painted important women in her life; specifically, women that she sees some of herself in.

I loved the symbolism, getting to talk to her, and hearing her story. It showed me that one of the amazing things about art festivals like this is getting to hear the story and intent behind a piece. That interaction made those pieces and the festival itself more special and memorable.
