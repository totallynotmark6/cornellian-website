On April 8th, as students wrapped up their finals and finished their Block 7 courses, Student Symposium returned for the first time since the 2022–2023 academic year, at which point it was canceled due to low attendance and lack of interest. Recognizing the event’s valuable contributions to the Cornell community, Student Senate fought to bring it back to campus. And this year, attendance was no issue: with 62 students presenting their work across 17 departments and programs, the 2026 Student Symposium was a major success.

Student Symposium is an opportunity for students to present their research, senior capstones, and other scholarly projects in front of the Cornell community. Participation in Student Symposium is a degree requirement for many programs at Cornell, as being able to succinctly and effectively present ideas and information to a general audience is a necessary skill for anyone to have, regardless of their academic discipline or career trajectory. The event also allows for professors and faculty members to celebrate the accomplishments of their students and see their projects come to fruition.

The symposium was held on the night of April 14 in Hall-Perine with over 65 attendants.
