Breaks are a time to have fun and relax, but there is also important information that Cornell College students need to be aware of. The campus provides information on dining service changes over break, residence hall practices, and travel support that can be useful to know before students head into both Fall and Winter break.

## Dining

Dining services from Bon Appétit will end over both Fall and Winter break. Over the Fall break, The Hilltop Café will be closed from Saturday, November 22, until Sunday, November 30 during lunch hours. Zamora’s Market will have reduced hours from November 22 to Wednesday, November 26 and offer a single meal service between 11:30 a.m. and 3 p.m. Flex dollars will remain available during these times. Zamora’s will be closed from Thursday, November 27 to Monday, December 1.

Over Winter break, Bon Appétit will temporarily end their services. The Hilltop Café will be open until after breakfast on Thursday, December 18. An important thing to note is that the last meal through the meal plan will be dinner on Wednesday, December 17. On December 18, students will have to pay at the door or have a sponsoring department. The Hilltop Café will resume its regular services starting with dinner on Sunday, January 11. Zamora’s Market will be closed from noon on December 17 to January 11, and will resume regular service hours on January 12.

## Residence Halls

Before break starts, there are several things to be aware of for students living in the residence halls. Over Fall break residence halls will remain open, and Resident Assistants will be on duty on break with reduced staffing.

Over Winter break, residence halls will be closing at 11 a.m. on December 18 and will reopen at 9 a.m. on Saturday, January 10. Health and safety checks will be conducted over Winter break by Residence Life staff. Some parking lots will be closed over Winter break—refer to campus safety for details.

Regardless of when you leave, turn off your lights, unplug any appliances, close and lock windows and blinds, lock your doors, and take out your trash.

## Travel

Over Fall and Winter break, shuttle services to the Cedar Rapids Airport are provided by Intercultural Life. There are QR codes at the Info Desk in Thomas Commons to reserve your spot on a shuttle. Additionally, check the resources linked under this article for easy access to the forms.

The roads may be hazardous due to weather, and it is smart to be aware of the conditions before you travel. For a detailed map for road conditions please check the Iowa Department of Transportation’s Iowa 511 website.

If you have any car troubles while on campus, call Campus Safety at (319) 895-4299 for assistance.
